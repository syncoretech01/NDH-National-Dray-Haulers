04 — Specialized

Overweight & Hazmat Containers

Heavy commodities such as metals, stone, beverages and machinery routinely exceed legal weight on a standard chassis. NDH runs tri-axle chassis, secures Virginia overweight permits and routes on approved corridors. Our hazmat-endorsed drivers move placarded cargo with the required documentation and placarding.

  • Tri-axle chassis for containers up to permitted gross
  • Virginia overweight permit acquisition
  • Hazmat-endorsed, TWIC-credentialed drivers
  • Placarding and shipping paper compliance
  • Scale-verified axle weights before departure
  • Routing on permitted heavy corridors
