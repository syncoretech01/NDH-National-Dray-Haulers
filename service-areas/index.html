Origin terminals

Every Port of Virginia gate, plus the rail ramps.

NDH drivers hold credentials and reservation-system access for each facility below, and dispatch monitors terminal advisories, gate hours and vessel schedules throughout the day.

NIT

Norfolk International Terminals

The port's largest terminal, with north and south gates, semi-automated stacks and the highest share of NDH import pulls.

Norfolk, VA
VIG

Virginia International Gateway

Automated terminal in Portsmouth handling ultra-large vessels. Appointment-driven gates make reservation discipline critical.

Portsmouth, VA
PMT

Portsmouth Marine Terminal

Multi-use terminal supporting containers, project cargo and offshore wind staging on the Elizabeth River.

Portsmouth, VA
NNMT

Newport News Marine Terminal

Breakbulk and ro-ro focused facility on the Peninsula with periodic container and specialized cargo needs.

Newport News, VA
RMT

Richmond Marine Terminal

Inland barge terminal on the James River linked to Hampton Roads by the 64 Express barge service, serving central Virginia receivers.

Richmond, VA
VIP

Virginia Inland Port

Intermodal rail facility in Front Royal connecting the port to the Shenandoah Valley, Northern Virginia and the I-81 corridor.

Front Royal, VA
