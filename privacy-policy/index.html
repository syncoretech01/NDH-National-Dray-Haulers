Privacy Policy

Effective September 1, 2026 · National Dray Haulers LLC

National Dray Haulers LLC ("NDH", "we", "us") respects the privacy of our customers, drivers, applicants and website visitors. This policy explains what information we collect through nationaldrayhaulers.com (the "Site"), how we use it and the choices you have.

Information we collect

Information you provide

  • Quote requests and contact forms: company, name, title, email, phone, shipment details (origin, destination, commodity, container and booking numbers) and any documents you attach.
  • Credit applications: legal entity details, federal EIN, business and billing addresses, ownership and officer information, accounts payable contacts, bank and trade references, requested credit terms, supporting documents such as a W-9 or financial statements, and the electronic signature of an authorized officer.
  • Driver and employment applications: name, contact details, CDL class and state, endorsements, TWIC status, driving experience, safety history, equipment information for owner-operators, resume and license copies you choose to upload.

Information collected automatically

When you visit the Site we receive standard technical information such as IP address, browser type, device type, pages viewed and the referring page. We use this data to operate and secure the Site and to understand how it is used. We do not use third-party advertising trackers. The Site uses session storage to remember that the loading animation has already played during your visit.

How we use information

  • To respond to quote requests, prepare rates and provide drayage services.
  • To evaluate credit applications, including contacting the bank and trade references you provide and obtaining business credit reports as authorized in the application.
  • To evaluate driver applications, including motor vehicle records, Pre-Employment Screening Program reports, drug and alcohol clearinghouse queries and employment verification as required by FMCSA regulations and as authorized by you.
  • To communicate about shipments, invoices, applications and account matters.
  • To comply with legal, regulatory, insurance and safety obligations.
  • To protect NDH, our customers and the public against fraud and misuse.

How we share information

We do not sell personal information. We share information only as needed to provide our services and run our business, including with: terminal operators, ocean carriers, railroads and chassis providers as required to move your containers; credit bureaus, banks and trade references in connection with credit applications; background screening, drug testing and compliance vendors in connection with driver applications; insurance carriers and legal advisors; and IT providers who host and secure our systems under confidentiality obligations. We may also disclose information when required by law or to protect our rights.

Data retention and security

Form submissions and uploaded documents are transmitted over encrypted connections and stored in access-restricted systems. Credit application records are retained for the life of the customer relationship plus the period required by tax and accounting rules. Driver application records are retained in accordance with FMCSA driver qualification file requirements. Other inquiries are retained as long as needed to respond and for reasonable business records. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Your choices

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal information we hold about you, subject to legal retention requirements, by emailing privacy@nationaldrayhaulers.com. You can opt out of non-essential email communications at any time by replying with "unsubscribe" or using the link in any marketing message. Virginia residents may have additional rights under the Virginia Consumer Data Protection Act; contact us to exercise them.

Children

The Site is intended for businesses and adults. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The effective date at the top of the page reflects the most recent revision. Continued use of the Site after a change constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
