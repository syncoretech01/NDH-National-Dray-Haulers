Terms & authorization

Please read and sign. Typing your name constitutes an electronic signature under the federal E-SIGN Act.

Credit terms. Upon approval, National Dray Haulers LLC ("NDH") will extend credit on the terms stated in the approval letter, typically net thirty (30) days from invoice date. Applicant agrees to pay all invoices in full by the due date without deduction or offset except for amounts disputed in writing within ten (10) days of the invoice date.

Late payment. Balances not paid when due accrue a finance charge of 1.5% per month (18% per annum) or the maximum rate permitted by law, whichever is lower. Applicant is responsible for all reasonable costs of collection, including attorneys' fees, if an account is placed for collection.

Accessorial charges. Applicant acknowledges responsibility for demurrage, per diem, chassis, storage, detention, tolls, permits and other accessorial charges incurred on its shipments in accordance with NDH's published accessorial schedule and any rate agreement in effect.

Credit investigation. Applicant authorizes NDH to obtain credit reports and to contact the bank and trade references listed in this application, and authorizes those references to release information regarding Applicant's payment history. NDH may adjust or withdraw credit at any time in its discretion.

Certification. The undersigned certifies that they are authorized to bind the Applicant, that the information provided is true and complete, and that the Applicant will notify NDH promptly of any material change in ownership, financial condition or contact information.

Electronic signature preview. By typing your name you agree this is the legal equivalent of your handwritten signature.

Submitting this application does not create an obligation for NDH to extend credit. You will receive a confirmation email with your reference number.
