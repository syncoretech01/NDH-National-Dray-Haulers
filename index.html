Intermodal drayage, done properly

Every container that lands at Hampton Roads has a clock on it. Free time, chassis days, appointment windows, rail cut-offs. We built NDH to beat that clock — with terminal-fluent dispatchers, drivers who know the gates, and equipment we control.

NDH is an asset-based drayage carrier headquartered in Virginia. We are at the Port of Virginia every day, pulling loaded imports, returning empties, positioning exports for vessel cut-off and shuttling boxes to the Norfolk Southern and CSX intermodal ramps.

Our lanes run from the Hampton Roads terminals throughout Virginia, up the I-95 corridor to Baltimore, Philadelphia and New Jersey, west along I-64 and I-70 into Ohio, Indiana and Illinois, and south through the Carolinas into Georgia and Tennessee.
