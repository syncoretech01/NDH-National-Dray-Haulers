Our story

We started NDH because we were tired of watching good freight get stuck behind bad drayage. Missed appointments, chassis nobody could find, phones nobody answered. Fixing that turned out to be a business.

National Dray Haulers is an asset-based intermodal drayage carrier headquartered in Northern Virginia with daily operations across the Hampton Roads terminals. We move import and export containers for beneficial cargo owners, freight forwarders, NVOCCs, brokers and 3PLs, and we do it with equipment we own and people we employ.

The founders came out of terminal operations and carrier dispatch. That background shaped everything: a chassis fleet we control, a dispatch desk staffed around the clock, and reporting built for the people who actually manage containers, not for a marketing brochure.

We are a Virginia company through and through. Our drivers live in the communities they serve, our trucks return to our yards every night, and our commitment to the Port of Virginia is the reason customers trust us with the lanes that matter.

Values

What we hold ourselves to.

Four commitments that decide how we hire, how we dispatch and how we treat a customer when something goes wrong.

01

Own the container

From availability to empty return, the box is our responsibility. We do not hand off problems, and we do not blame the terminal.

02

Tell the truth early

If a load is going to be late, you hear it from us first, with a plan. Bad news delivered fast is the most valuable thing a carrier can offer.

03

Respect the driver

Maintained equipment, predictable home time and pay that lands every Friday. Drivers who are treated well treat your freight well.

04

Measure everything

Terminal turns, on-time percentage, accessorial rate, driver retention. If it affects your container, we track it and we publish it to you.

Milestones

From two trucks to a regional network.

2016

Founded in Virginia

NDH opens with two day-cab tractors and six chassis, running imports from Norfolk International Terminals to Richmond and Northern Virginia receivers.

2018

UIIA and full terminal coverage

Interchange agreements with the major ocean carriers, credentials at every Port of Virginia terminal and the first dedicated import program for a regional retailer.

2020

24/7 dispatch and owned chassis fleet

Round-the-clock dispatch desk launched during the pandemic volume surge; chassis fleet expanded past 60 units to break dependence on depleted pools.

2022

Overweight and Midwest lanes

Tri-axle chassis program and Virginia overweight permitting added; regular lanes established to Ohio, Indiana and Illinois for manufacturing customers.

2024

Secure yard and transload network

Container storage yard commissioned in Hampton Roads, with pre-pull, chassis staging and partnerships with port-adjacent transload facilities.

2026

140+ chassis, 17 states

Fleet exceeds 140 chassis and 65 tractors, with direct drayage coverage across the Mid-Atlantic, Northeast, Midwest and Southeast and milestone visibility for every customer.
